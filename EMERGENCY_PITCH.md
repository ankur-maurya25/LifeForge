# LifeForge: 25-to-30 Second Emergency Pitch

Use this rapid elevator pitch when judges have very limited time (less than 1 minute).

---

## ⚡ Spoken Script (Target Time: 28 Seconds)

> *"Hi judges! Most to-do apps fail because they feel like corporate spreadsheets—people lose motivation and quit.*  
> 
> *We built **LifeForge**—a web platform that turns personal productivity into an **RPG adventure**.*  
> 
> *Your goals become **Nemesis Bosses**, your daily tasks become **Combat Strikes**, and every completion awards **XP**, advances your **Level**, and defends your **Daily Streak**.*  
> 
> *Powered by **React**, **Node.js**, **Express**, and **MongoDB**, LifeForge makes discipline visual, addictive, and rewarding.*  
> 
> *Forge your discipline. Slay your procrastination. Thank you!"*

---

## 🎯 4 Rapid Speaking Anchors
1. **Problem:** To-do lists feel like boring spreadsheets.
2. **Solution:** Goals = Bosses; Tasks = Combat strikes.
3. **Loop:** Tasks → XP → Level Up → Defend Streak.
4. **Tagline:** Forge your discipline. Slay your procrastination.
